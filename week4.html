Nuclear War: The Rising Risk, and How We Stop It Reflection

Assignment 04 • May 22nd, 2026

Nuclear War: The Rising Risk, and How We Stop It made me think more seriously about nuclear war. Before reading this article, I thought of nuclear war as something from the past, mostly connected to history books, movies, or the Cold War. I knew nuclear weapons were dangerous, but I did not really think about them as a current issue. This article showed me that nuclear war is still a real risk today, especially because of conflicts and tensions happening around the world.

One thing that stood out to me is how much damage even one nuclear weapon can cause. Sometimes people talk about “small” nuclear weapons, but the article made it clear that there is really nothing small about them. A nuclear weapon can kill many people, destroy cities, cause radiation, and leave damage that lasts for a long time. This made me realize that nuclear weapons are different from regular weapons because their effects do not end right away. They can continue hurting people and the environment for years.

I also really liked the visuals as I scrolled through the page. The article was not just plain text, and that made it more powerful. The visuals helped me understand the danger in a stronger way. Instead of only reading about nuclear war, I could also see images and movement that made the topic feel more real. Since nuclear war is such a big and scary topic, the scrolling visuals made it easier to understand emotionally. It made the article more impactful and harder to ignore.

Another idea that stood out to me is that people often describe nuclear war as “unimaginable.” I understand why people say that, because it is terrifying to think about. However, the article made me realize that not thinking about it can also be dangerous. If people ignore the risk, then they may not care enough about stopping it. This reminded me that serious problems do not disappear just because they are scary or uncomfortable to talk about.

The article also made me think about how much power political leaders have. A decision involving nuclear weapons could happen very quickly, but the consequences would affect millions of people. This is scary because people can make mistakes, misunderstand each other, or act out of fear. It made me question whether any country should have weapons that can destroy so many lives at once.

After reading this article, I think nuclear weapons should be talked about more often. It is easy to assume that nuclear war will not happen because it has not happened in a long time. But this article showed me that preventing nuclear war takes effort and awareness. I do not think regular people can fix this problem alone, but we can still learn about it and take it seriously. This reading made me feel worried, but it also made me understand why awareness is important. Nuclear war is a scary topic, but ignoring it is not the answer.
