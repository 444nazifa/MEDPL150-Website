The Forgotten Forest

The Forest · A Younger Self

The girl on the platform was her.

The child sat at the edge of the platform with her feet hanging over the tracks.

She was small. Maybe seven or eight. Her coat was too thin for the rain, and her shoes were covered in mud.

She did not look like a ghost.

She looked tired.

Like she had been waiting for someone who kept choosing not to come back.

The woman stepped closer.

The child did not turn around, but she spoke.

“You forgot me.”

For a moment, the forest stopped breathing.

Choose carefully. The train remembers.

Tell her, “I remember now.” You accept the memory. Sit beside her and say, “I’m sorry.” You stop abandoning her. Turn away before she looks at you You leave the memory behind again.
