The Perfect Life

The Rooftop · Everyone Knows

They all acted like they already loved her.

Someone placed a glass in her hand.

Someone else called her by name before she had said it. They laughed at her jokes before she finished them. They remembered stories she had never told.

Everyone here wanted her.

Everyone here understood her.

Everyone here smiled the same way.

At first, it felt like being accepted.

Then she noticed the smiles did not change. Not when the music stopped. Not when the wind knocked over a glass. Not even when she asked, quietly, “How do you know me?”

The guests kept smiling.

Like they had been waiting for that question.

Choose carefully. The train remembers.

Let yourself be loved here You choose the beautiful lie. Ask why everyone knows your name You break the fantasy. Return to the train You leave before the party becomes permanent.
