The Forgotten Forest

The Forest · The Backpack

Everything she had forgotten was inside.

The backpack opened too easily, like it had been waiting for her.

Inside was a faded photograph of a little girl standing on this same platform. The girl’s hair was tangled from rain. Her shoes were muddy. She was holding a train ticket in both hands.

There was a note folded beneath the photo.

The handwriting was careful and uneven.

Dear me,

If you find this, please do not leave me here again.

Her hands started to shake.

The girl in the photograph was her.

Choose carefully. The train remembers.

Take the photo and find the child You accept that this memory belongs to you. Close the backpack You choose not to remember. Return to the forest station You step back before deciding.
