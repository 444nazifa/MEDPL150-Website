Branch 01 · The Quiet City

Nobody looked at her. Nobody spoke.

The streets stretched in every direction, shining with rain under old amber streetlights.

A traffic signal changed from red to green for no one. Store windows glowed, but every room behind them was empty.

At first, the silence scared her.

Then, slowly, it started to feel safe.

Here, no one could ask what was wrong. No one could expect her to explain herself. No one could leave her, because no one had come close enough to stay.

Then she saw movement at the end of the street.

A figure stood in the fog, facing her.

For the first time in this city, someone noticed.
