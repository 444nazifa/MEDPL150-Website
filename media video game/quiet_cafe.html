The Quiet City

The Empty Café · Warm Light

She was the only customer. But it felt like being watched.

The café smelled like coffee and rain.

A single cup waited on the table near the window. Steam curled from it even though no one had touched it.

Behind the counter, a waitress stood perfectly still.

The chairs were all pushed in. The music playing from the old speaker was soft and familiar, like a song she used to hear when she was safe.

The waitress finally looked up.

“You can stay,” she said. “No one will ask you to leave.”

The rain tapped against the glass.

For the first time in a long time, the room felt warm.

That was what made it dangerous.

Choose carefully. The train remembers.

Stay where nothing can hurt you You choose comfort over the truth. Leave before the room becomes a cage You reject comfort and keep searching. Return to the train You are not ready to choose yet.
