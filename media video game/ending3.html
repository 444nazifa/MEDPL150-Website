Return to the train

Ending 03 · The Surface

She carried the memory out with her.

The way out was not bright at first.

It was only a staircase, wet with rain, leading upward through the dark.

Behind her, the station was quiet.

The child’s voice was gone. The false city had gone quiet. The train waited somewhere below, breathing in the tunnel like it was alive.

She understood now.

The places on the line had never been separate. They were all pieces of what she had tried to escape: the loneliness, the lie, and the memory.

She could not erase them.

She could not leave them behind like they belonged to someone else.

So she carried them.

The memory still hurt. The truth still felt heavy. But it was hers now, and that made it different.

At the top of the stairs, morning waited.

Not perfect.

Not painless.

Real.

When she turned around, the train was gone.

For the first time, leaving did not feel like forgetting.

Choose carefully. The train remembers.
