The Quiet City

The Alley · No Exit

The figure disappeared into the alley.

She followed before she could think.

The alley narrowed with every step. Brick walls leaned inward. Fire escapes crossed above her like ribs. The figure stayed ahead, always the same distance away.

Not running.

Not waiting.

Just leading.

When it passed beneath a broken streetlamp, she finally saw enough to understand.

The figure had her coat.

Her walk.

Her shape.

It was not a stranger. It was what she would become if she stayed invisible long enough.

The figure turned the corner and vanished.

The alley went silent.

At the end of the alley, a train door opened by itself.

Choose carefully. The train remembers.

Follow the shadow to the front of the train You stop running from what you became. Turn back before you vanish too You are not ready to face this version of yourself. Return to the train You leave the shadow unanswered.
