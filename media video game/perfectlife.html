Branch 02 · The Perfect Life

Everything felt exactly right.

Too right.

The city was beautiful in a way that made her chest ache.

Every streetlight glowed at the perfect brightness. Every window showed a life she almost wanted. Every face turned toward her with an easy smile.

A couple laughed as she passed. A stranger waved like they had known her for years. Somewhere above the street, music drifted from a rooftop party.

This was the life she might have designed for herself if someone had asked what happiness should look like.

But no one had asked.

And still, the city had known.
