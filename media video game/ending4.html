Ending 04 · The Conductor

At the front of the train, she found herself driving.

The front car was waiting for her.

No matter which path she took, it had been leading here.

The alley with no exit. The city that repeated. The forest that remembered her. Every road had pointed toward the same door.

Inside the conductor’s cabin, the controls glowed in the dark.

The map showed every station on the line.

The Quiet City.

The Perfect Life.

The Forgotten Forest.

All of them were still there.

All of them were hers.

She had spent so long being carried from one fear to another that she forgot to ask who was driving.

Now she knew.

The train had never been lost.

It had been waiting for her to take the controls.

She placed her hand on the lever.

The track ahead disappeared into blackness, but this time, the dark did not choose for her.

She pushed forward.
