Wake on the train

Ending 02 · Perfect Enough

She chose the dream with open eyes.

The perfect city welcomed her back like nothing had happened.

The lights glowed. The music played. The people smiled and called her name with voices full of warmth.

She saw the cracks now.

She saw the same faces repeating in different windows. She saw the same street turn back into itself. She saw the same laugh begin before the joke was told.

It was not real.

But it was kind.

It gave her the life she wanted without asking what it had cost.

So she stopped searching for the mistake.

She smiled when the others smiled. She raised her glass when they raised theirs. She let the city remember a version of her that had never been hurt.

The loop closed gently around her.

This time, she did not fight it.

Choose carefully. The train remembers.
