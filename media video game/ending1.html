Wake on the train

Ending 01 · The Warm Room

She stayed until the rain forgot her name.

The café light never went out.

The rain stayed soft against the windows. The cup stayed warm between her hands. No one asked where she had been. No one asked what she remembered. No one asked her to be brave.

For a while, that felt like peace.

The waitress moved quietly behind the counter. The music played the same gentle song every night. Outside, the city blurred into amber light and fog.

She knew the train was still out there.

Sometimes she heard it beneath the floor, passing through the dark with its doors open.

But she did not stand.

Not yet.

Maybe comfort was not freedom.

Maybe it was only another kind of room.

Still, she closed her eyes and let herself rest.

Choose carefully. The train remembers.
