Branch 03 · The Forgotten Forest

The station smelled like rain and old memories.

The station had been swallowed by trees.

Vines wrapped around the turnstiles. Ferns pushed through the cracks in the platform. Moss covered the benches, but the station sign still hung above the tracks.

She knew this place.

Not from a dream.

Not from a nightmare.

From before.

Somewhere deeper in the station, a child laughed.

On one of the benches sat an old backpack, soaked with rain but untouched by time.

Her hands went cold.

Choose carefully. The train remembers.

Search the old backpack You may find what you tried to forget. Follow the child’s voice You may meet the part of yourself you abandoned. Leave before you remember You avoid the memory and return to the train.
