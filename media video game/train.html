Station 00

Night Line · Terminal

You are alone on the train.

The seats are wet, but the windows are dry.

Outside, there is no city. No tunnel. No sky. Only darkness passing by like something alive.

A map glows above the doors. Three stations pulse on the line. Each one feels less like a destination and more like a question.

The train does not move until you choose.

Choose carefully. The train remembers.

The Quiet City Face the part of you that wants to disappear. The Perfect Life Face the lie you wish was real. The Forgotten Forest Face the memory you left behind. Return to start Begin again before the train remembers you.
